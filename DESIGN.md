## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-conviction, introspective digital sanctuary. It merges the deliberate editorial cadence of literary publications with the precision and interactive fluidity of modern psychometrics. The experience balances psychological authority with warm revelatory discovery: users are not taking an arbitrary quiz; they are uncovering their intrinsic social signature.

The visual direction marries **Dark-Mode Editorial Minimalism** with **Luminous Glassmorphism**. Dark obsidian foundations anchor the space, removing environmental noise and heightening focus. Archetypal discoveries emerge through calibrated radial glows and crystalline surface tiers, making interpersonal insights feel tangible, rare, and deeply validating. The aesthetic communicates poise, emotional intelligence, and enduring gravitas.

## Colors

The palette operates on an anchored dark-slate spectrum illuminated by six dedicated archetype accents. 

### Foundation Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`):** Obsidian midnight velvet, absorbing glare and driving maximum focus into typography and data.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#111827`):** Deep charcoal slate for ambient card tiers and assessment panels.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E293B`):** Raised interactive containers, modals, and input states.
- **Structural Stroke Default (`#1F2937`):** Fine architectural bounds between content sections.
- **Structural Stroke Highlight (`#374151`):** Active card borders, dividers, and selected states.

### Archetype Signature Spectrums
1. **The Lion (`#F59E0B` / Amber Gold):** Commanding authority, solar presence, resolute leadership.
2. **The Fox (`#F97316` / Copper Tangerine):** Strategic wit, rapid agility, perceptive adaptability.
3. **The Dolphin (`#06B6D4` / Radiant Teal):** Empathetic resonance, fluid sociability, warm connectivity.
4. **The Owl (`#6366F1` / Royal Indigo):** Intellectual depth, architectural contemplation, quiet precision.
5. **The Peacock (`#D946EF` / Magnetic Fuchsia):** Unapologetic expression, charismatic pull, bold storytelling.
6. **The Bear (`#10B981` / Anchor Emerald):** Grounded stability, steady calm, unconditional trust.

### Functional Typography Values
- **Text High-Emphasis (`#F9FAFB`):** Headlines, scores, and primary statements.
- **Text Medium-Emphasis (`#94A3B8`):** Assessment questions, explanations, analytical microcopy.
- **Text Low-Emphasis (`#64748B`):** Metadata, question indices, unselected options.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional juxtaposition between classical editorial literature and surgical clarity:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes the authoritative gravity of psychological journals and bespoke monologues. Used for archetype titles, assessment declarations, and primary narrative reveals.
- **Body & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its crisp geometric aperture, ensuring that complex psychometric prompts, likert scale options, and multi-paragraph personality breakdowns remain effortless to digest without optical fatigue.
- **Labels & Badges (`Plus Jakarta Sans` Uppercase / Semi-Bold):** Adds methodical structure to trait percentages, archetype markers, and assessment metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a focused, rhythmic progression built around a centered reading experience.

### Grid Systems
- **Assessment Flow:** A single-column, distraction-free container locked to a maximum width of `760px`. This isolates introspective decisions and centers cognitive bandwidth.
- **Results & Dashboard:** A 12-column responsive grid with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters on desktop, reflowing to a 6-column grid on tablet and 4-column stack on mobile.
- **Max Canvas Width:** Capped at `1280px` for multi-column dashboards, maintaining a high-end editorial ratio surrounded by obsidian letterboxing.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Margin Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Outer canvas margins of `3rem` (`48px`). Generous vertical pacing between assessment steps (`space-xl` * 2) preserves an unhurried, contemplative rhythm.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Margins drop to `2rem` (`32px`), gutters scale to `1.25rem` (`20px`). Radar charts and archetype cards shift from side-by-side to stacked modular sections.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** Margins compress to `1.25rem` (`20px`), gutters to `1rem` (`16px`). Assessment answer options convert from horizontal comparison chips to full-width tactical tiles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is produced using atmospheric luminance rather than aggressive drop shadows. Surfaces appear as suspended smoked crystalline panes hovering over the obsidian void.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#0B0F17` backdrop. Ambient radial gradients mapped to the user’s primary archetype softly disperse across the upper quadrant (`blur(120px)`, opacity `12%`).
- **Level 1 (Smoked Glass Cards):** Background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle, high-precision perimeter border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating Elements):** Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Diagnostic Layers):** Raised floating panels resting on top of a heavy dark veil (`rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.85)` with `blur(8px)`).

### Luminous Ambient Glows
Shadows are tinted with the relevant archetype's color value rather than neutral black:
- **Active State Glow:** `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(accent_hex, 0.25), 0 0 0 1px rgba(accent_hex, 0.45)`.
- **Resting Depth:** `0 16px 40px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined balance of geometric clarity and soft edges:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for inputs, tactical buttons, and scale indicators.
- **Card Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to assessment options, psychometric modules, and content enclosures.
- **Dashboard & Modal Containers (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Envelops primary personality dossier cards and comparative radar views.
- **Capsule / Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved strictly for archetype category badges, status chips, and primary floating call-to-action triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Dynamic archetype-tinted fill or commanding amber (`#F59E0B`) with `#0B0F17` typography for immediate optical prominence. Features a pill silhouette, subtle inner highlight bevel, and slight luminous spread on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent obsidian fill with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text in `#F9FAFB`. On hover, the border brightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)` with a background lift to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

### Assessment Likert & Selection Cards
- Large, tactile card options built as interactive glass panels.
- **Resting:** `1px solid #1F2937`, `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.6)` background. Text remains `#94A3B8`.
- **Hover:** Border shifts to `#374151` with an elevated background of `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`.
- **Selected:** Border takes the active archetype accent color (`1.5px solid [accent]`), with an interior radial wash (`rgba([accent], 0.08)`), switching body text to pure `#F9FAFB`.

### Archetype Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pill containers.
- Surface: `rgba([accent], 0.12)` background accompanied by a micro-border `1px solid rgba([accent], 0.3)`.
- Typography: `label-sm` uppercase, tracking `0.08em`, colored in the full archetype hex. Includes a leading 6px glowing dot beacon.

### Inputs & Assessment Prompts
- Dark recessed surfaces (`#0B0F17`) bounded by `#1F2937`.
- Focused state applies an ethereal halo using the secondary archetype accent (`#6366F1`) with zero harsh stepping.
- Caret is tinted to the primary accent color.

### Hexagonal / Radar Chart Profiles
- Web-rendered SVG charts built with delicate concentric poly-rings styled in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Trait vectors represented by thin hairline rules ending in archetype-colored node points.
- The user’s polygon silhouette is rendered with an archetype gradient fill (`opacity: 0.25`) framed by an ultra-crisp `2px` luminous vector boundary.

### Archetype Reveal Dossier (Signature Card)
- A high-impact, collector-card style container framed in double-layered glass.
- Top section holds a high-resolution ambient archetype icon or symbol paired with `headline-2xl` serif typography.
- Sub-traits, shadow sides, and communicative strengths are rendered as modular segmented bars featuring smooth linear progress transitions.
